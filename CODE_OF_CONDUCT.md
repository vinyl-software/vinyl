# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want Vinyl to be a friendly, respectful and constructive community.

Whether you're reporting a bug, suggesting an improvement, or helping someone else, please treat everyone with respect.

## Expected Behaviour

* Be respectful and considerate.
* Keep discussions constructive and focused on improving Vinyl.
* Provide useful and honest feedback.
* Assume good intentions.
* Respect different opinions and levels of technical knowledge.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviour is not acceptable:

* Harassment, bullying or personal attacks.
* Discriminatory or hateful comments.
* Threats or intimidation.
* Sharing private or personal information about others.
* Deliberately disruptive or abusive behaviour.
* Spam or inappropriate promotional content.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or see unacceptable behaviour, please report it by opening an issue or contacting the project maintainer privately where appropriate.

Issues and discussions may be closed or removed if they don't follow this Code of Conduct.

Thank you for helping make the Vinyl community a welcoming place.
